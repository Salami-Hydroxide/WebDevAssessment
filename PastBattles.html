Bigfoot

Nessie

Mothman
Despite having the advantage of flight, if caught, the mothman would be unable to escape the grip of the Bunyip, getting dragged into the water and drowning.
Bunyip
Since the Bunyip is said to be between 5 and 15 feet it would have the advantage in size, and if it is able to grab the Mothman at least once it would be as good as dead.
Mongolian Death Worm
Despite the Mongolian Death worm being around 2 feet long it is described to be “so poisonous that merely to touch it means instant death”, making it clear who would win between these two cryptids.
Jersey Devil
Despite having the size advantage of about 3 to 7 feet, one touch of the worm would mean instant death, making it unlikely that the Jersey Devil would win.
